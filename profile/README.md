## [About Us]()

𖤐 [**Mindful AI  Research**]()  is an independent, open-source research and innovation lab advancing human-centered artificial intelligence through applied research, technological development, interdisciplinary collaboration, and responsible innovation.

Our work explores machine learning and data science, computer vision, geospatial intelligence, intelligent agents, conversational AI and large language models (LLMs), as well as emerging and decentralized technologies.


Founded by  [Fabiana ⚡️ Campanari](https://linktr.ee/fabianacampanari); a multidisciplinary professional with academic backgrounds in Design, Software Development, and Psychology, and currently pursuing undergraduate studies in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at PUC–SP (Pontifícia Universidade Católica de São Paulo), the lab brings together perspectives from technology, human behavior, cognition, design, and semiotics.
  
This interdisciplinary foundation informs how we investigate the relationship between people and intelligent systems: how people perceive, interpret, communicate, and interact with technology. We bring human insight into the design, development, evaluation, and practical application of AI-driven solutions.

Our approach places particular emphasis on responsible AI, transparency, explainability, algorithmic accountability, and technology governance. We consider relevant regulatory and data-protection frameworks, including the EU AI Act, Brazil's LGPD, and the GDPR, where applicable.

By combining technical rigor, experimentation, reproducibility, and open-source collaboration, we connect research with practical innovation—turning ideas and complex challenges into meaningful technological solutions.

We are also proud to highlight PUC–SP's Data Science and Artificial Intelligence program, which received a [five-star rating](https://j.pucsp.br/noticia/guia-da-faculdade-2024-puc-sp-possui-sete-cursos-com-cinco-estrelas) in the 2024 Guia da Faculdade.

Mindful AI Research brings together a growing open-source community of 42+ contributors (community figure to be kept current), connecting people, ideas, and technologies to build solutions that empower people and serve humanity.

## Responsible AI and Governance

We approach [**AI governance by design**](), considering responsibility, transparency, accountability, security, and human oversight throughout the AI lifecycle.

Our work is informed by:

<br>

[-]() Brazil's AI policy and applicable national requirements <br>
[-]() The European Union AI Act <br>
[-]() Data-protection frameworks, including Brazil's LGPD and the GDPR <br>
[-]() International responsible-AI principles and best practices

<br>

 *We aim to develop and promote systems that are:*

<br>

- [**Explainable**](): designed to make AI-generated outputs and decisions more understandable. <br>
- [**Auditable**](): supported by appropriate documentation, evaluation, and accountability. <br>
- [**Secure**](): developed with attention to security and data protection. <br>
- [**Fair**](): evaluated for potential bias and unequal impacts. <br>
- [**Human-centered**](): designed around human needs, values, and meaningful oversight.

<br>

> [!NOTE]
>
> *Governance is not a separate afterthought. It is part of how responsible systems are conceived, built, evaluated, and maintained.*

<br><br>

### Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)

Our GRC perspective translates responsible-AI principles into practical considerations, including:

- Risk identification, assessment, and mitigation
- Regulatory alignment and documentation
- Monitoring, evaluation, and lifecycle oversight
- Transparency, explainability, and accountability
- Evidence-based assessment and reproducible practices

<br>

The objective is to support intelligent systems that can be understood, evaluated, and responsibly managed.
